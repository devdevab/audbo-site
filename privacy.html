Privacy Policy

audbo · Effective November 2026

audbo is built to be private by design. We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.

Your Library Stays on Your Device

All books, PDFs, comics, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, margin notes, and reading statistics are stored strictly on your iPhone. audbo has no user accounts, no cloud servers, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

On-Device Voice Narration

audbo's Listen feature uses the open-source Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) on-device neural text-to-speech model (~330 MB). You can pre-download the voice pack during onboarding, from Library → Offline Voice, or the first time you tap Listen. The model files download once from Hugging Face directly to your iPhone. After that one-time download, all audio generation runs 100% locally on your device — your book text is never transmitted to any server.

Optional Cover Art Lookups

If an imported book does not contain an embedded cover image, audbo may query the Apple iTunes Search API or the Open Library API using only the book's title and author to fetch cover artwork. No personal identifiers or reading habits are sent.

Local Wi-Fi Transfer

When you open Send from Mac, audbo runs a temporary local web server strictly inside your local Wi-Fi network so you can transfer files directly from your computer to your iPhone. Files never pass through external servers, and the server shuts down as soon as you close the sheet.

Backups & Exports

When you back up your library or export highlights as Markdown, audbo creates a local file that you save or share using standard iOS system sheets. You remain in complete control of those files.
