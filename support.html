How do I add my books?

Tap + Add Books in the Library to import DRM-free EPUB, PDF, CBZ, CBR, or CB7 files from the Files app or iCloud Drive. You can also choose Send from Mac in the Library menu to drag and drop books directly over your local Wi-Fi network.

How do offline neural voices work?

You can pre-download the offline voice pack (~330 MB) during onboarding, from Library → ••• → Offline Voice, or by tapping the headphones icon while reading. Audbo downloads the open-source Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) voice model directly to your iPhone. Once downloaded, all 6 studio voices run 100% offline — including with your screen locked.

How do I use two-page open-book mode on iPhone Duo?

Unfold your iPhone Duo and hold it horizontally like a book. audbo automatically switches to a two-page center-spine spread with 3D page turns. Fold the device or hold it vertically to return to single-page reading.

How do I back up my library or export notes?

Open the Library menu (…) and choose Back up library to create a portable backup file you can save anywhere. To export highlights and notes from a book, open the book's Notes view or Highlights tab and tap Export Markdown.
